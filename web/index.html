01 / SET THE FOUNDATION

A little about your fit.

Put the flat reference beside your body, at the same distance from the camera. Its measured edge must be vertical and fully visible. Do not hold it closer to the camera. A4 and US Letter are different sizes.

Leave height blank if unknown in reference mode. If entered, use a measured height without shoes. Weight is not required and is not inferred from a photo.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN
  • Wear close-fitting clothes.
  • Keep your head, hands and feet in view.
  • Face a level camera; stand straight with arms slightly away from your sides.
  • Use even lighting and a clear background.

Your photo stays in your browser. Only landmarks, height inputs and calibration marks are sent to calculate estimates. No photos or measurements are saved on the server.

02 / CAPTURE YOUR PROPORTIONS

Step into the frame.

FRONT VIEW

A good fit begins with a clear view.

Start your camera or choose a full-body photo.

Choose your height source, prepare the reference if needed, then start a capture.

Mark your full height

First mark the top of your head and the floor. In reference mode, then mark the TOP and BOTTOM of the known vertical edge. Or use the sliders below. Scale depends on correct placement and exact reference length.

Reference object marks

The orange segment must follow the object's measured vertical edge. Adjust both X and Y for each endpoint if using sliders.
