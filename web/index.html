01 / SET THE FOUNDATION

A little about your fit.

Use your actual measured height, without shoes. We never assume a height.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN
  • Wear close-fitting clothes.
  • Keep your head, hands and feet in view.
  • Face a level camera; stand straight with arms slightly away from your sides.
  • Use even lighting and a clear background.

Your photo stays in your browser. Only body landmark coordinates and height are sent to calculate estimates. No photos or measurements are saved on the server.
